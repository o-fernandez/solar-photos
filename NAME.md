# The Name: Solar

## Short version

The product is **Solar**. The repo is `solar-photos`. The soul of it is a Taíno word: **güey**, the sun.

Photos are light. Solar is where your light lives — on your machine, in your hands, owned by you.

## The name

**Solar.** It works on three layers at once:

- **Light.** A photograph is captured light. Solar is the home for it. Naming the
  app after the sun ties it directly to what a photo *is*.
- **Home.** In Spanish, *casa solar* is the ancestral family home — the rooted
  place where a family's memories live across generations. That's exactly what a
  photo library is: the place your people and your moments are kept.
- **Sound.** Short, instantly sayable in English and Spanish, easy to type, easy
  to remember. No friction at the point of contact.

## The soul: güey

Beneath "Solar" sits the real root. **Güey** (pronounced *gway*) is the Taíno word
for **the sun**. The Taíno are the Indigenous people of Puerto Rico, and güey was
held as the source of all creation and life — also carrying meanings of strength
and good fortune. The sun symbol appears in petroglyphs carved across the island,
like El Sol de Jayuya.

This matters because the maker is Puerto Rican, with roots near Mayagüez, and the
project is partly an act of ancestral reconnection. The Taíno were declared extinct,
but the language and identity persist — *el sol es Taíno*, the sun is Taíno. Naming
the app's soul after güey is a quiet way of carrying that forward.

So: **Solar on the surface, güey underneath.** The clean, universal name reaches
people easily; the Taíno root gives it meaning and lineage. Most users meet "Solar"
first and discover güey when they read about it — and that discovery is the point.

## Why this layering (the decision behind it)

We deliberately chose *not* to name the repo or product "güey" directly, despite its
deeper meaning. The pronunciation and the unfamiliar diacritic would put friction on
every new person's first encounter — and the audience reach matters. Instead:

- **Surface (Solar):** frictionless, sayable, ownable. What people type and share.
- **Soul (güey):** the origin story in the README and about page. What people
  discover and remember.

The ancestry lives one layer down, on purpose. It informs the identity without
taxing every new user.

## How it should show up in the app

- Product name everywhere user-facing: **Solar.**
- The README and about screen open with the güey story — photos are light,
  *el sol es Taíno*, this is where your light lives.
- Tone: warm, rooted, unpretentious. Never corporate. The name carries heritage
  lightly, not as a museum plaque.

## One-line essence

> **Solar** — a local-first photo library where your light lives. Named for *güey*,
> the Taíno sun.
